New Apostolic English High School and Dr. Bower Apostolic Junior College, operating under the National Apostolic Church Education Society, are committed to protecting the privacy of everybody who uses this website. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and what we do with it.

## What we collect

We collect information only when you choose to give it to us. That happens in four places on this website:

- **Admission enquiry form** — parent or guardian name, email address, mobile number, the student's name and date of birth, the grade sought, your city and current school if you provide them, how you heard about us, and any message you write.
- **Contact form** — your name, email address, an optional phone number, a subject and your message.
- **Career application form** — your name, email address, mobile number, the position applied for, your qualification and experience, an optional link to a CV or profile, and your covering note.
- **Newsletter signup** — your email address only.

We do not use advertising cookies, we do not run third-party analytics that profile you across other websites, and we do not sell, rent or trade personal information to anybody.

## Why we collect it

We use the information you submit for one purpose: to respond to you. An admission enquiry is used to contact you about admission to the school. A career application is used to consider you for the position. A newsletter address is used to send school updates and nothing else.

We do not use enquiry data for unrelated marketing, and we do not pass it to third parties for their own purposes.

## Where it goes

Form submissions are delivered directly to the school office by email, or to a secure notification endpoint controlled by the school. This website does not operate a public database of enquiries, and submissions are not stored on the website itself.

Our website is hosted on Vercel, and pages are served over an encrypted HTTPS connection. Standard server logs — which may include an IP address and browser type — are retained by the hosting provider for security and diagnostic purposes.

## How long we keep it

Admission enquiries are retained for the duration of the admission cycle they relate to, and then for as long as required for the school's own records. Career applications are retained for the recruitment round they relate to, and for a reasonable period afterwards in case a similar vacancy arises. Newsletter subscriptions are retained until you unsubscribe.

## Children's information

Where an enquiry concerns a child, the information is provided by a parent or guardian on the child's behalf. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children through this website.

## Cookies

This website uses a small number of strictly necessary items stored in your browser, including your display preferences — theme, text size, contrast and motion settings. These are stored locally on your own device, are never transmitted to us, and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.
