Welcome to New Apostolic English High School and Dr. Bower Apostolic Junior College — an institution that has become, over the years, a milestone of academic excellence and extra-curricular achievement, and one that has enlarged and enriched the education available in South Nagpur.

Ours is a school with a healthy and harmonious atmosphere. That is not an accident of good fortune. The credit belongs to the blessing of our Founder, Bishop Adv. Dr. Vincent S. Bower, and of Mother Mrs. Alice V. Bower; to a management that has never asked the school to be anything other than serious about children; and to the diligence of learned teachers who give far more than the timetable requires of them.

We are proud of the vibrant learning environment we have built here. Every child is encouraged to explore, to discover, and to reach their full potential — not the potential someone else has decided they should have. A classroom in this school is a place where a question is welcome, where a mistake is the beginning of understanding rather than the end of it, and where effort is noticed as clearly as attainment.

Our goal is straightforward, and we hold ourselves to it. We are building a community of lifelong learners, equipped with the skills, the confidence and the moral compass to thrive in a world that will keep changing long after they have left us.

To the parents who trust us with their children: thank you. That trust is the foundation the whole institution rests on, and we do not take it lightly. To the students reading this: work hard, be kind, and be curious. The rest follows.
