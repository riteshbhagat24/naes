The plantation drive opens the school's environmental programme for the session. Students across sections plant saplings on and around the campus — and, more importantly, are each made responsible for one of them for the year that follows.

Watering rotas are kept by the class, and survival rates are reported back at the end of the session. Environmental responsibility is taught here as a habit with consequences rather than as a topic with a chapter.

Students should bring a hat and a water bottle. Gardening tools and saplings are provided.
