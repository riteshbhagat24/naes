The annual Science & Tour Exhibition is where the year's practical work goes on public display. Students from the middle school upward design and build a working model, prepare a short explanation of the principle behind it, and defend it in front of parents, staff and visiting schools.

Alongside the models, students who have been on the year's study tours present their findings — photographs, measurements, interviews and what they concluded from them.

Every exhibit is student-built. Teachers supervise; they do not assemble. Parents are welcome throughout the day, and the physics and computer laboratories are open for viewing.
