Independence Day is one of the two mornings in the year when the whole institution — Pre-Primary through to the degree programmes — stands on the same ground at the same time.

The morning opens with flag hoisting and the national anthem, followed by a march past in which every section takes part. Students then present patriotic songs, a short address and a display drill prepared over the preceding fortnight. Parents are welcome; please arrive by 7:15 AM so that the ceremony can begin on time.

Students should be in full formal uniform. Class teachers will confirm assembly points and timings for each section by circular.
