On Teachers' Day the senior students take the timetable. Class XI and XII students are assigned periods across the primary and middle school, prepare a lesson, and teach it — an exercise that changes how a sixteen-year-old thinks about the adults at the front of the room.

The afternoon is given to the students' programme for the staff: songs, sketches, a tribute from each section, and the presentation the school has been rehearsing quietly for weeks.

No special dress is required. Ordinary school uniform, ordinary school day, extraordinarily different atmosphere.
