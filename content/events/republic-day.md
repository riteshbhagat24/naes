Republic Day is marked with flag hoisting, the national anthem and a short cultural programme prepared by the senior sections.

The morning also carries the announcement of the house standings for the year to date, and the presentation of certificates to students who have represented the school in inter-school competition during the session.

Students should be in full formal uniform and assembled by 7:15 AM.
