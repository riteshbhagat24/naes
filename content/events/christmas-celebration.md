Christmas at New Apostolic is a whole-school occasion. The Pre-Primary and Primary sections present the nativity, the choir leads carols, and the high school and junior college contribute the closing items of the programme.

It is also the morning the school's motto is read aloud and returned to — love thy neighbour as thyself — which is the reason the celebration has never been treated as an optional extra here.

Parents and families of all faiths are warmly welcome.
