The Annual Cultural Programme is the evening the school works towards from November. Each of the four sections stages a full production — recent titles have included *Aasman Se Gira*, *Choo Manter* and *Laher Badlav Ki* — with students responsible for the performance, the choreography and a large part of the staging.

It is the clearest illustration of what we mean by an all-round personality: the child who cannot be persuaded to answer a question in class will, given a role and eight weeks, hold a hall of four hundred people.

Tickets are not required. Seating is allotted by section; details follow by circular.
