Apostolic Sports is the largest day in the school calendar. The morning opens with the march past and the oath, moves through the track and field finals contested by all four houses, and closes with the relay and prize giving.

Events run across athletics, throwball, volleyball, basketball and the primary section's own programme of races. Indoor finals in carrom and table tennis are decided in the week before the meet.

As in previous years, the full meet is streamed live from the school ground so that families who cannot attend in person can watch every event.
