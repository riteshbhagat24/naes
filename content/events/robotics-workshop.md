A hands-on robotics workshop for high school classes, run in the computer laboratory. Students work in small teams to wire a sensor, write the control logic and make a build respond to its surroundings without further instruction.

The value of the session is in the failures: a design that looks correct on paper and does not work on the floor teaches more in an afternoon than a chapter does in a week.

No prior programming experience is required. Students should bring a notebook; everything else is provided.
