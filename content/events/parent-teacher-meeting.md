A scheduled meeting with your child's class teacher and subject teachers ahead of the final term assessments.

Come with questions. The most useful conversations are the specific ones — a subject that has slipped, a habit that has changed at home, a plan for the months before the board examination. Progress records, practical journals and assessment papers are available for review at the meeting.

Slots are allotted by section to keep waiting short. Your child's class teacher will confirm your time by circular.
