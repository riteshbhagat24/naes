Students from the high school section represented the school at a state-level throwball fixture — the strongest sporting result on the school's record, and the outcome of a season of practice on our own ground.

Throwball has become one of the school's strongest disciplines, alongside athletics, carrom and table tennis. Physical education is timetabled for every grade, and inter-house competition runs through the year, which is what makes a state-level side possible in a school of our size.

Our congratulations to the team and to the staff who coached them.
