High school classes completed a practical robotics programme run in the school's computer laboratory, working in small teams to wire sensors, write control logic and make a build respond to its environment without further instruction.

The programme was designed around failure. Teams tested on the floor, watched their design behave differently from the way it behaved on paper, and went back to the logic — which is a more accurate picture of engineering than any diagram.

Robotics now runs each session for high school classes alongside the science and tour exhibition.
