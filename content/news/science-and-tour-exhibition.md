The annual Science & Tour Exhibition brought the year's practical work into public view. Students from the middle school upward designed and built working models, explained the principle behind each one, and answered questions from parents, staff and visiting schools.

Alongside the models, students presented findings from the year's study tours — photographs, measurements and the conclusions they had drawn from them.

The physics and computer laboratories were open throughout the day. Every exhibit on the floor was student-built.
