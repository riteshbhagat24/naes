The achievement of students at Dr. Bower Apostolic Junior College was carried as a news feature by UCN News Live, Nagpur — recognition earned entirely by the students themselves.

The junior college is deliberately small. Lecturers know whether a student attended, whether they understood and whether they have submitted, and that is not a claim every institution can make. Practical papers are supervised in the computer laboratory through the year rather than assembled in the final week.

The full feature is available in our [video gallery](/videos). Our congratulations go to the students and to the faculty who taught them.
