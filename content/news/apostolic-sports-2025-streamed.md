The annual Apostolic Sports meet was streamed in full from the school ground, start to finish, so that parents, grandparents and families living away from Nagpur could watch every event as it happened.

The broadcast ran the length of the meet — the march past and oath, the track and field finals contested across the houses, the primary section's races, the relays and the prize giving.

The decision to stream came from a simple observation: a sports day is one of the few occasions in a school year when a child is visibly, publicly at their best, and a working parent should not have to miss it. The recording remains available in full on our [video gallery](/videos).
