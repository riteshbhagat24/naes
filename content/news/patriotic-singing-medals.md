Students who entered the inter-school patriotic singing competition returned to the school with medals and participation certificates.

Music and elocution are entered for inter-school competition every session, and the preparation is treated exactly as sport is: auditions, a rehearsal schedule and a coach who expects the work to be done between sessions.

For most of these students, the competition is the first time they have performed in front of an audience that is not their own school — and that experience is worth as much as the medal.
